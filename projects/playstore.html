Google Play Store – Product Health & App Quality Analysis

An end-to-end analysis of 10,357 Google Play Store apps and 37,427 user reviews, built to identify which app categories carry the highest quality and retention risk using a composite Risk Score across rating, abandonment, and sentiment.

Overview

The Problem

App marketplaces host thousands of apps across dozens of categories, but not all categories are equally healthy. Some carry chronically low ratings, high abandonment, and heavy negative sentiment, while others thrive. Without a structured way to compare categories side by side, it is difficult for a product or business team to know where to focus a limited quality and retention budget.

This project builds a full analytics pipeline, from raw data cleaning through SQL analysis to an interactive Power BI dashboard, culminating in a composite Risk Score that ranks all 33 categories by overall health.

Methodology

How It Was Built

The full pipeline runs in three phases, from raw data to a ranked, interactive dashboard:

1. Data Cleaning
2. SQL Analysis
3. Power BI Dashboard

Data was cleaned in Excel, removing duplicates, handling missing ratings, and dropping irrelevant columns across both the apps and reviews datasets. SQL queries in SQLite used GROUP BY, CASE WHEN, JOIN, CAST, and REPLACE to build category-level health metrics, update-recency trends, and a full review sentiment breakdown across all 33 categories.

A composite Risk Score measure was built in DAX, combining abandonment rate, negative review percentage, and average rating into a single ranked metric, then visualized in a conditional-formatted table alongside supporting charts on a three-page Power BI dashboard.

Output

Results and Findings

BUSINESS emerged as the highest-risk category overall, driven by the lowest average rating (2.59), the steepest abandonment rate (36.77%), and a high negative review share (16.4%). GAME showed the single highest negative-review rate of any category (38.28%), but its stronger rating (4.10) and much lower abandonment (4.19%) kept its overall risk in check, suggesting its complaints reflect narrower friction points rather than fundamental product failure.

Apps last updated in 2018 rated 15% higher on average than apps last updated in 2016, pointing to update recency as a leading indicator of app quality.

Recommendation: prioritize a full quality-and-retention audit for BUSINESS first, since it is the only category failing on all three risk dimensions simultaneously, while treating GAME's sentiment spike as a narrower, targeted fix.
